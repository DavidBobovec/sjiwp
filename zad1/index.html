Whether competing or cheering, expect legendary matchups, rising stars, and an unmatched atmosphere.


History was made last year. Now it’s time to level up.

ABOUT

In 1996, 40 players gathered in Sunnyvale to battle it out over arcade classics Super Street Fighter II Turbo and Street Fighter Alpha 2. Though we’ve evolved beyond the days of arcade cabinets, that same competitive spirit remains.


We’re not just a convention. We’re a global community of more than 10 million casual gamers, esports enthusiasts, and industry professionals dedicated to building a bigger world for fighting games.
